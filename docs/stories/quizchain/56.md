## A stray link

"AOI Second." The answer is `AOH`, and the field is `I after H`.

The author typed a stray `link` into the hash, right where the link placeholder sat. It held 19 days. The claim came 36 minutes after the author published the answer and the typo.

The fallout: the second run would start with a 77 mBTC block, as a penalty for this one.
