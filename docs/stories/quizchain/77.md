## The genesis address

77 mBTC, the final block of the first run, funded on April 29 and posted on May 3. "The solution is the puzzle."

The puzzle is the genesis address, which the author credits to Satoshi. The answer is eight items: four read from the address's left half, four from its right half backwards. Then `TOMI Omit TOMI`, because the author hashed a capital O by mistake, and the whole block 44 key.

It took ten hints and two and a half months. On July 15, 2019 a player posted `1A1 efi2DM Hal Finney TS mN ihsotaS otomakaN`, and the author confirmed it. The claim had confirmed four minutes earlier.

Is that really a message from Satoshi about Hal Finney? The author doesn't know either, and says so.
