## A before B

"BItcoin Second." The field is called BFUB now, brute force user block. The FU in the middle is purely technical, of course.

The first letter of Bitcoin is B, and the letter before it is A. So `A BFUB Before B fTS`. Almost four hours for a one letter answer. Compare that with blocks 25 and 26, which went in minutes, and you see what the field was for.
