## Two eras

"Two words with harmony." The hint says it was big news in Japan a week earlier. That's the new era name, Reiwa, announced in April 2019.

Showa and Reiwa both pair a kanji with 和. Those two kanji mean luminous and command, so the string is `luminous command xhm`. Players tried Reiwa for hours and kept getting only half of it.

It held twelve hours. The next post owns up: most players aren't native Japanese speakers, so no Japanese themes for a while.
