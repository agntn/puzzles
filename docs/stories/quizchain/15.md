## The hash app reset itself

A sentence from the whitepaper, minus some letters. Which letters? The ones in the author's handle: a, o and i. So `The lngest chn nt nly serves s prf f the sequence f the events wtnessed ZNb`, with a space before the link this time.

The post said SHA-256. Then came the emergency update. The author had restarted the hashing app, it woke up on MD5, and nobody noticed. The funded key is the MD5 one.

An MD5 hash is 16 bytes, so the seed has 12 words instead of 24. Everything else is the same. And that accident stuck: from here on the whole chain runs on MD5.
