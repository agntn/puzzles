## Why Nakamoto?

"Why Nakamoto?" 67 mBTC, locked behind the road block of 66.

Why sounds like Y, and Y is Atbash territory. Naka is middle, moto is origin. So `Atbash Middle Origin`. The author's own reading: Nakamoto comes from the MN pair in the middle of the Atbash alphabet. Since the author is not Satoshi, they have no idea if that's true. They just liked it.
