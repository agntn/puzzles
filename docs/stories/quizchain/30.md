## The first BotFU

After block 27 the author wanted bots to pay. So the format grew a field: the answer, `BotFU`, a short decisive hint, then the link.

The question was `4231`. It took the Sphinx riddle, a nod to block 1 and a pile of hints to get to `Humanity First BotFU Sphinx Yang JRu`. Forty hours. The author even misprinted the first hash digits, and the winner spotted it.

The verdict in the update: way too much complexity for humans. The field stays, but only where a short answer needs it.
