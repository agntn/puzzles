## The other impossible one

"We found out that this is the second" plus six words and a period. Like block 13, it needed the final Wattpad chapter, published months later. The post printed this key's tail, `mph`, so nobody had to wait.

In the final chapter the sentence ends in `climate emergency episode on this planet.`. The claim came on July 22, 2019. A player then posted the string, the MD5 and the WIF, which is where the record's key comes from.
