## 11-9

May 18. The question is just `11-9`. Format `[solution] TOMI [TOMI]`, prefixes `923`, `0` and `6`. The author expected it to be "solved at sight".

It wasn't. Players tried subtraction, Roman numerals, Atbash, block 2's key. One player announced "Solved!" with `119 TOMI november top posted`. The prize didn't move. Hint 1 a day later: the idea came from a Wikipedia article. Hint 2 the day after: the article is about the number 9.

K is the 11th letter. 11-9 is K-9, said like "canine". So the answer is `K-9` and the TOMI field is `police dog`. Good boy.
