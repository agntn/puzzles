## Bat, twice

The trick is [block 5](/collections/quizchain2/5). Same idea, five words then: `bat bot bit bet but`. Every vowel between a `b` and a `t`. Now it's block 52, so the first word goes in twice: `bat bat bot bit bet but`.

Why `bat`? Because it's two words, the one from Batman and the baseball one. The author calls that a homonym, and that's the excuse for six.

The funding and the claim confirmed in the same block, at **14:42:25 UTC**, an hour and 44 minutes after the post. u/kimi_tousan explained it right after. u/BrainForceOne liked the groundwork, not the twist. Checking a doubled word by hand? Painful.
