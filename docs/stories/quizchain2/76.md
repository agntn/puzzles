## change to

July 22, 2019. The last big 77 mBTC block. "Maybe of medium difficulty." The question is two words, `change to`. Format `[solution] TOMI [TOMI]`, digits `f8e`. The author planned to shut down soon after, so no hints and no questions.

That didn't last. "Started up again", and with it the digits for the solution alone, `1d`. Then the one hint: change the question to `from change to`. On August 3 somebody asked if it's "to" or "two". The answer was one word. `to`.

## The guess that matched twice

u/martypyouknowme tried `format TOMI before TOMI`. The full MD5 starts with `f8e`. The MD5 of `format` alone starts with `1d`. Both checksums pass. The address doesn't. "Got excited when I saw the MD5 hash of f8e. But no cigar." Brutal.

## Seven years later

Then nothing. For seven years the 77 mBTC sat there. The claim finally confirmed at **09:31:23 UTC** on August 17, 2026. "Seven years later, congratulations to the solver", wrote u/JDScreesh a few days after.

Who swept them? Still nobody's said. The answer came from somewhere else entirely.

## A typo gives it away

On October 20, BTCxanter posted a script in a pull request to floflo777's open-crypto-puzzles repo, with block 76 marked `NO SOLUTION`. On October 31, eyaler replied with [two small typos](https://github.com/floflo777/open-crypto-puzzles/pull/49#issuecomment-6542211466). One replaced that placeholder with `EMERGENCY TOMI climate EMERGENCY`. BTCxanter later fixed the file.

The answer is shouting. The solution is `EMERGENCY`, and the TOMI field is `climate EMERGENCY`. Change to what, though? Blocks 71 to 74 are all about climate. In May 2019, the Guardian began preferring "climate emergency" over "climate change". Change to, indeed.

The full MD5 starts with `f8e`, and the solution's starts with `1d`. Better still, its twelve BIP39 words derive the claim's public key at `m/44'/0'/0'/0/0`. The chain backs this reproduction. The record now carries the key, cites eyaler's comment for the entropy, and says `solved`.
