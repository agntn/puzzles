## HI there

June 9, 2019. The question is `HI there`. The solution is two pairs of capital letters. And the TOMI field starts in the post: `Atbash foldover`, then four more words. Only one digit block, `836`. The author expected a fast solve.

Fast? It sat there for almost a day. u/Crypto_Rachel posted page after page of letter pairs and their meanings. Players asked for more digits, and the author posted `1` for the solution and `42` for the TOMI field.

## Two to the right

Same alphabet as [block 30](/collections/quizchain2/30), folded in two rows. Block 30 read one place to the right. This one reads two. Start at H and I, step two to the right, and you get `HQ` and `IP`. The TOMI field spells it out: `Atbash foldover two to the right`.

The claim confirmed at **21:58:04 UTC** on June 10, an hour before the first hint. u/BrainForceOne explained it later. The TOMI hash did most of the work. And it paid `1Thanksg9t4MRoCH2CM3cArWq26JiNvMY` again, the address from [block 5](/collections/quizchain2/5).
