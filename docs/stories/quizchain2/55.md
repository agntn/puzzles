## Be afraid

June 30, 2019. The first 77 mBTC block since [block 14](/collections/quizchain2/14), the bonus for the block 53 mistake. The question: `Be afraid. Be very afraid.` Digits `48a`, nothing else. The author expected it to be easy. How easy?

## Fear of missing out

The answer is `FOMO`, and the TOMI field is `fear of missing out`.

u/Randomiser had it two minutes after the post. The claim confirmed at **23:03:40 UTC**, under four minutes after the post. "It was solved even before the tweet!" wrote u/JDScreesh.

The winner agreed it was too easy for a big block. The author too: "Maybe I should have done the big block later." So players asked for another one, for the same mistake. They got it, as [block 57](/collections/quizchain2/57). Not bad for FOMO.
