## Twenty-one ones

June 26, 2019. The post starts with an apology for block 49 and its leaked TOMI field. No extra prize for that mistake, though. Then the question: `111111111111111111111`. Twenty-one ones. Digits `c32`, `8` and `64`.

Just a number? Not quite.

## The checksum

Twenty-one ones are a valid Bitcoin address, almost. A block explorer completes it to `1111111111111111111114oLvT2`. The missing part is the checksum. So the answer is `4oLvT2`, and the TOMI field is `address checksum`. The author found the trick in an old Bitcointalk post about address checksums.

u/BrainForceOne explained the solve right after the claim confirmed, at **06:51:06 UTC**, under three hours after the post. The claim paid their `1Thanksg9t4MRoCH2CM3cArWq26JiNvMY` again. "This one was a good one", wrote another player. Hard to argue.
