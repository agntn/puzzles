## Cross sum

May 24, 2019. The title says 7 mbtc, the post says 8. Why? "There is a number seven in 17", and the cross sum of 17 is 8. Sure, why not.

The question: `500 years in the right eye`. Format `[solution] TOMI [TOMI]`, digits `885`, `a` and `f`.

## LV

The Mona Lisa is about 500 years old. Sources say there are tiny letters in her right eye, `LV`. The author's update says Leonardo signed his work there. So the string is `LV TOMI Mona Lisa`.

The post went up at 22:58:40 UTC. The claim confirmed at **23:05:03**. Six and a half minutes. u/mooncritic posted the string, then asked if the extra 1 mBTC was a mistake and offered to send it back. Nice. The 8 was on purpose, right there at the top of the post. The 7 in the title? The author called that "another mistake". No penalty block for it, though.

Why so fast? u/silver_anth explained it: paste the question into Google and the answer is the first thing you see. The author admitted to googling it before posting. "Maybe not careful enough..." Fair enough.
