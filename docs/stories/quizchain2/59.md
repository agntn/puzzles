## Atbash, two to the right

Two days and two hints later: Atbash foldover. Write A to M over Z to N. Start at E, move two to the right to G, look down. T. The answer is `ET`, and the TOMI field is `Atbash foldover two to the right`, the same as [block 34](/collections/quizchain2/34).

The claim confirmed at **23:18:08 UTC** on July 6. And then u/Quantris noticed. The hash starts with `9be`, not `9b3`. The author typed the digits by hand. u/JDScreesh posted the whole hash, `9be70160646f1c10dbec577ebf20f715`.
