## A vanity address

May 28, 2019. The question is an address: `1AndrewYangForPresident2o2o6zmPzd`. The format is `[solution]`, digits `497`. The author expected it to be a bit harder than block 19.

It wasn't. Nobody holds the key to an address like that. Coins sent there are gone for good. So the solution is just `burn address`.

The claim confirmed at **04:10:09 UTC**, twelve minutes after the post. u/Randomiser posted the answer once it confirmed. u/MegaBit01 solved it too, tried to sweep with Electrum and got an error. The coins were already gone.

## The swapped characters

u/mapl3sn0w spotted something. The real address ends in `zm6Pzd`, and the question says `6zmPzd`. A typo? The author said it had no influence on the solution.

u/Quantris made a sharper point. Swap two characters and the checksum breaks, so the address in the question isn't valid at all. You can't burn anything to it. Checked here: `@agntn/keys` rejects the question's version and accepts the one with `zm6P`.

So the answer to "what is this?" is technically "not an address". Still `burn address` on the chain, though, because that's what the hash says.
