## Warm miners

A day passes. The hints say Superman leads to Satoshi, meaning Hal Finney, and that the URL depends on your hardware. The author used a phone. The last one is `warm miners`.

The answer is a Jameson Lopp tweet with Hal Finney in a Superman shirt: `https://mobile.twitter.com/lopp/status/1143879778170232833`. The mobile one. u/silver_anth had seen the tweet before. The desktop link just hashes to something else.

The claim confirmed at **08:21:06 UTC** on June 30, 29 hours after the funding. "I would consider the solution a mistake", wrote the winner. The author agreed. So [block 55](/collections/quizchain2/55) got 77 mBTC. Fair trade?
