## A dirty word, safe for work

Block 3 went up 18 minutes after block 2, back at 7 mBTC. The question: "dirty word with XA". The post promises "No, not that kind of dirty word, this is safe for work." The link is the first run's block 76 key again.

So what's XA? Atbash. Flip the alphabet, A becomes Z, X becomes C. XA is CZ, as in CZ of Binance.

## Reorg

Binance had just been hacked, and there was talk of a Bitcoin chain reorg to undo it. CZ later apologized for even using that "dirty word". So the solution is `reorg` and the TOMI field is `CZ Binance`.

u/Pr0m3th3on solved it. Their first puzzle ever. The claim confirmed at **02:12:14 UTC**, and the comment says the claiming part took longer than the solving: their wallet was at home and they were on a work laptop. "VICTORY IS MINE." Fair.

The author's verdict? Without the Atbash twist it would have been too obvious. And for a harder version, the TOMI field could have been `Atbash CZ`.
