## A number

July 7, 2019, 7 pm Japanese time. The luckiest slot of the whole run, so block 61 pays 77 mBTC. Partly luck, partly an apology for the wrong digits in [block 59](/collections/quizchain2/59). The question is `A number.` Format `[solution] TOMI [TOMI]`, digits `06d`, and `4b` for the TOMI field alone.

"May even be solved at sight", says the author. Was it? Not even close. Three days of guessing follow.
