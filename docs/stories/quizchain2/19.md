## Cypherpunks

What came before Bitcoin? Players tried the obvious list. Hashcash, DigiCash, Bit Gold, b-money. All projects, all wrong.

The answer is the people. `Cypherpunks`. The mailing list where a lot of the earlier digital cash ideas were discussed, and the mindset Bitcoin comes from, as the author put it. Same values as PGP and Tor.

The claim confirmed at **08:53:31 UTC**, 56 minutes after the post. u/mooncritic posted the answer and said they almost gave up. A human, then. The author was pleased.

At the 24 minute mark the author wrote that it "survived 20 minutes", as if it were gone. "n/m...still there," a player replied. It lasted another half hour.
