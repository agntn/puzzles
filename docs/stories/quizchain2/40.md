## Famous mistake

June 16, 2019. The question: `Famous mistake.` One word in lower case, and a TOMI field of one word. Only one digit block, `5a9`. Both parts are too easy for more.

A famous mistake in Bitcoin? There's only one.

## hodl

The answer is `hodl`, and the TOMI field is `hold`. Solved at sight.

Then the race. Several players tried to claim at the same moment, and the author called it luck who got confirmed first. Somebody posted the answer before the claim confirmed, and u/puzzleponky said it got double spent. The claim that won confirmed at **13:52:40 UTC**, under an hour after the post. It paid a full 1 mBTC in fees. Hodl, but pay the miner.
