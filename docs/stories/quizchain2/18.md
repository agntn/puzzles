## Halvening

Bitcoin's block reward halves every 210,000 blocks. 50, then 25, then 12.5. The third halving makes it `6.25`, and in May 2019 that was still a year away. The TOMI field is the word for the event, `Halvening`.

The claim confirmed at **16:52:37 UTC**, about four hours after the post. Nobody on Reddit posted the string. u/puzzleponky said the winner was a human who isn't on Reddit at all.

u/BrainForceOne had `6.25` and still missed. They spelled it `Halving`. Same event, different word, wrong hash. That's TOMI for you.
