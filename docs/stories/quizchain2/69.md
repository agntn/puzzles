## Waste of time, sir

July 15, 2019. The question is a running gag. `"Waste of time, sir."`, a line players post whenever a block goes wrong. Format `[solution] TOMI [TOMI]`, digits `a8f`.

Under the post, the author does the math. 838 mBTC in prizes for the first run, 1145 for the second, 777 more for stage two of block 77. "Probably will have wasted my money, but it might just move something."

## Madam

After five hours, partial hashes: `be` for the solution, `07` for the TOMI field. And `07` is the same as yesterday.

u/puzzleponky tried `girl` and `I am a lady` first. Then the TOMI hash rang a bell: `palindrome` again, straight from [block 68](/collections/quizchain2/68). The answer is `madam`. Polite, female and a palindrome. Who calls the author sir?

The claim confirmed at **13:51:25 UTC**, under six hours after the post. Brute forced, some said. The author believed the winner.
