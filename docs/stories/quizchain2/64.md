## JD6

July 10, 2019. A simple one, the author hopes. The question is `JD6`. Format `[solution] TOMI [TOMI]`, the TOMI field is one word in lower case, digits `215`.

No partial hashes this time. Too easy to script, says the author. Is it?

## Six keys to the right

Atbash turns `JD` into `QW`. Six letters starting with QW? `QWERTY`. The TOMI field is `keyboard`. Or `kayboard`, as the first version of the update had it. A commenter caught that. "I am crazy, but not THAT crazy."

u/BrainForceOne explained the solve a few hours later. JDJDJD, nothing. Caesar, nothing. Atbash, and the keys sit next to each other. Six keystrokes to the right gave QWERTZ, the Swiss layout. One extra twist and it's QWERTY. AZERTY players probably had no chance.
