## Five words, five vowels

May 14. "Five words." One space between them, no period. MD5 prefix `e7a`. No TOMI, no link. The author checked the entropy and thought it was safe.

u/BrainForceOne had it in 25 minutes: `bat bot bit bet but`. How? Block 49 of the first run used `hat hot hit`. They'd already wondered whether it extends to every vowel. "So I had the solution in my mind before block 5 was posted." Sixth try.

The author's favorite detail: the "bit" in Bitcoin works with almost every vowel. Only Y is missing.
