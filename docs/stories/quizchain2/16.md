## A hard name from 2012

Block 16 went up a minute after [block 15](/collections/quizchain2/15), on May 24, 2019. Block 15 was a lottery. This one is meant to be harder, and it is.

The question: `Difficult name found in 2012.` The format is `[solution] TOMI [TOMI]`. The capitalization "will be obvious if you find the correct method", and the TOMI field is "a string of 9 random characters". Digits `bbc`, and later, by popular demand, `3` for the solution alone.

Then came hint 1, a bit early: "9 random characters TOMI field and 9 characters name."

## ELiZABETH

Found? Yes, as in found by a vanity address generator. In 2012 someone found `1ELiZABETHdzGTuQeg2RnCcWBMVo3ySfTz`, a record nine characters long. The name is `ELiZABETH`, odd lower case i included. The nine random characters after it are the TOMI field.

So the string is `ELiZABETH TOMI dzGTuQeg2`. The claim confirmed at **07:23:02 UTC** on May 25, and u/puzzleponky posted the answer five minutes later, with the Bitcointalk post where they found the address. They liked it. "A perfect question for the solution."
