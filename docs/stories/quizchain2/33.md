## Can you find the answer?

June 9, 2019. The question is the whole puzzle: `Can you find the answer?` A TOMI field too. Digits `b60`, `f` and `7`. The post even went up thirteen minutes before its funding transaction got a block.

Players called it impossible. u/silver_anth called it a guessing game. The author called it "very easy if you find the method". Both were right, in a way.

## Four hints, then 0078

The hints came slow, then fast. The TOMI field has four items and starts with three capital letters. The method came from a player's comment under block 27. The last item is `list`. And the solution is a four digit number.

So: where do you find the word `answer` as a number? In the BIP39 word list. It's on line 78. Hence `0078`, the format of the site the author used. The TOMI field is `BIP 39 word list`.

The claim confirmed at **05:23:23 UTC** on June 11, two days after funding, and u/Randomiser says it was theirs. And the zeros? u/puzzleponky noted the list counts from zero, which makes `answer` word 77. Would have been a nice number for this series.
