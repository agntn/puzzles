## Great deeds

June 5, 2019. The question is `Great deeds`. The solution is one word with a capital first letter, plus a TOMI field. Digits `72f`, `8` and `c`. Hints come fast this time, 20 and 40 minutes after the post.

The author asked what hint people wanted, got a split vote and tossed a coin. Hint one: "with little". Hint two: the TOMI format is `[name] [number]`.

## Riddle number 4

Search "Great deeds with little" and you find it. Riddle 4 of the hundred riddles of Symphosius.

> Great deeds with little strength I do,
>
> I close the open, ope the closed for you.

The answer is `Key`. What else would a Bitcoin quiz pick? The TOMI field is the author's name and the riddle's number.

The claim confirmed at **04:50:56 UTC**, 50 minutes after the post. Fast hints, fast solve.

## Symphonius or Symphosius?

The author's update says Symphonius. u/puzzleponky spotted it. The poet is Symphosius, "good thing you did not make that mistake in the actual puzzle". The author fixed the update.

The chain agrees. `Key TOMI Symphosius 4` hashes to `72f`, the digits the post gave, and derives the address the claim spent. `Key TOMI Symphonius 4` hashes to `d39`. Typo in the post, not in the puzzle. Phew.
