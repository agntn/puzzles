## FOG COOL

June 3, 2019. A 9 mBTC block, because 2 plus 7 is 9. The question is `FOG COOL`. The post gives the digits `c3d` only, since the TOMI field "follows clearly and uniquely from the solution". Players protested. The author added the first digit of the solution, `a`.

Theories poured in. u/martypyouknowme had `frozen creek`, because each word sits 27 places after `fog` and `cool` on the BIP39 list. Checked here: `fog` is 722, `frozen` 749, `cool` 382, `creek` 409. Beautiful. Also wrong.

## Atbash again

Hint one was one word: "Atbash." `FOG COOL` becomes `ULT XLLO`. Take the letters at the edges and you get `UTXO`. The TOMI field is `unspent transaction output`.

The claim confirmed at **13:59:06 UTC** on June 4, about four hours after the hint. u/userm1010110 posted `UTXO TOMI unspent transaction output`. Their first solved puzzle, after hours and hours on Quizchain.

u/Crypto_Rachel called it a wacky jump from `FOG COOL`. And the author? A coincidence, but a cool one, that FOG helped hide it.
