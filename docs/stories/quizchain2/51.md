## No question

June 26, 2019. Block 51 has a format, `[solution]`, and three digits, `4c4`. No question. No TOMI field either.

The author funded it right before posting, with a high fee. The idea was to keep the window short, so nobody claims the prize before the post is even up. Smart. Did it help?

## Eight seconds

Not really. Block 51, so `51 percent attack`. u/DanTheDon83 saw it coming and had the answer written down in advance. u/JDScreesh timed the claim at eight seconds after the post.

The funding and the claim confirmed in the same block, at **23:04:41 UTC**. Solve time on the chain: zero. The claim paid `16YUMnqAExU7yCCRaE1kfFrc4X9c4dMFjL`, the address from blocks 44 and 45. A script for the claim? No, Electrum, said the winner.

Not everyone cheered. "Please go back to your puzzles", wrote u/mapl3sn0w, and asked for real ciphers again. The author promised a harder one next.
