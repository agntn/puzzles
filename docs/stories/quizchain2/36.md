## de or ha?

June 12, 2019. Rapid fire hints split the players, so this one is slow again. The question is `de or ha?` No format hints, on purpose. Digits `1db`, `f` and `f9`.

## A common ending

No. `de`, `or` and `ha` all take the same ending. Debit, orbit, habit. The answer is `bit`, and the TOMI field is the three words. The connection to Bitcoin needs no explaining.

u/tdp94 posted `bit TOMI debit orbit habit`. The claim confirmed at **11:47:57 UTC**, under four hours after the post. Players liked it. "No unreasonable aspects", said u/Quantris. High praise in this run.
