## Atbash foldover

Write the alphabet in two rows, A to M on top and the rest folded back underneath. That's an Atbash foldover. Look straight down and you get pairs like `BY`. The author looked one place to the right instead and found `EU IQ KO`. With `AY` from the question, the block uses every vowel. And `IQ` fits a riddle.

The TOMI field is `Atbash foldover`. The claim confirmed at **20:37:36 UTC** on June 8, three days after funding. u/kimi_tousan posted the string and found the trick in an alphabet of 25 letters, not 26.
