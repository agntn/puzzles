## Odd

June 13, 2019. The question is `odd`. The answer is one word in capitals, the TOMI field four words in lower case. Digits `4f0`, `7` and `89`.

One hint, a day later. The third word of the TOMI field is `alphabet`. And the second digit of the answer's hash is not `3`. A nice way to stop people chasing the wrong idea. What do you do with a word like `odd`?

## ACE

The source is a tweet by Pieter Wuille. The vowels sit at odd positions in the alphabet. A, C and E are the first three odd letters in a row. So the answer is `ACE`.

u/Randomiser posted `ACE TOMI odd numbered alphabet letters`. The claim confirmed at **00:05:14 UTC** on June 15, about an hour after the hint.

## Numbers or letters?

The author's update says the TOMI field was `odd numbered alphabet numbers`. The chain disagrees. `ACE TOMI odd numbered alphabet letters` hashes to `4f0`, the posted digits, and gives the address the claim spent. The `numbers` version hashes to `b80`.
