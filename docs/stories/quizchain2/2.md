## A password manager from Wattpad

u/Quantris got it in under two hours. The claim confirmed at **02:22:37 UTC**. Then came the walkthrough, and it's a good one.

The author's Wattpad story has a chapter called "Quizchain as a Password Manager". Its example master string is `"BaSCifCatfAaa1i"Metamon`. Run it the way the chapter says. MD5 gives `2941774a…d1d53d91`. That's BIP39 entropy, and the wallet's second key is `L5Z66qPmUkTAsWQywjRNHDxHrX6J1X1SQedp6V8QsbaXR7rGd6ex`. That key is the solution. Hash it with MD5 again and you get the block's entropy, `7b44cc11…ad6795e1`.

Why the second key? Because it's block 2. One player said they tried exactly this "in the first 30 seconds", with the first key. Ouch.
