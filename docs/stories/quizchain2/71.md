## not zero

July 16, 2019. Block 71 pays 8 mBTC, the cross sum of 71. The first funding was only 7 mBTC, so the author sent one more. "No big block for this mistake..." Fair. The chain had its own joke, though: the 1 mBTC top-up confirmed one block before the 7 mBTC it was topping up.

The question is `not zero`. Format `[solution] TOMI [TOMI]`, digits `4bc`. A TOMI field again, on purpose. The last block went at sight, so this one should be harder.

## net zero

The guesses were fun. `Jordan TOMI Why joke`. `Zero No Tsukaima TOMI Hiraga Saito`. "Thought I was onto something", wrote u/martypyouknowme.

Half a day in, the author posted partial hashes, `48` for the solution and `bc3` for the TOMI field. That did it. The answer is `net zero`, one letter off the question, and a goal of climate policy. The TOMI field says the same thing again: `carbon neutral`.

The claim confirmed at **15:30:17 UTC** on July 17. Three minutes later u/LiaVl posted "Got it". And u/Quantris? They had it too, but wrote `carbon-neutral` with a hyphen. Correct English, wrong hash.
