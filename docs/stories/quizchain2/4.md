## A four letter word

May 13. "Looking for a four letter word. Yes, quite safe for work." Format `[solution] TOMI [TOMI]`, no link. The post says why: the solution and TOMI field have enough entropy on their own. The real reason comes later.

The prefixes: `9ac` for the whole string, `2` for the answer, `9` for the TOMI field. And a warning: "either very easy if you get the method or impossible if not."

## MasK

Look at the block 3 key. `L488KiS3jUut79rDM6gwxUdg7Qm1SXwQnMasKLpzFzb6KrNLsFpT`. See it? Right there, `MasK`. Once you see it, you can't unsee it.

That's why there's no link. With the block 3 key in the format, the word would jump out at you. The author had done this before, with `Hoax` and `hit` in first run keys. And this one fits the theme. Privacy was Bitcoin's original purpose, so a mask is a pretty good word to find.

u/puzzleponky had it in an hour. The claim confirmed at **13:57:44 UTC**. The full string is `MasK TOMI previous block private key`. They tried the whole key as the TOMI field first, expecting a twist. No luck, so a few more tries.

Nobody printed this key. The record derives it.
