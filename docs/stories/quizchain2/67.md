## An almost perfect anagram

July 12, 2019. Another 77 mBTC block. The cross sum would be 13, unlucky, so it's 77 again, like in the first run. The question: `Satoshi is an almost perfect anagram of which name?` Format `[solution] TOMI [TOMI]`, digits `f47`. No partial hashes, it's a big block.

Not solved at sight. Without the TOMI field, says the author, brute force would have taken it by now.

## Thomas

The answer is `Thomas`. And the TOMI field is Hal Finney's full name, as Wikipedia gives it: `Harold Thomas Finney II`. Hal again. u/puzzleponky found it with Wikipedia and a little help from [block 54](/collections/quizchain2/54).

The claim confirmed at **23:46:22 UTC** on July 12, 49 minutes after the post. Someone claimed puzzleponky was the author in disguise. "Try Occam's razor", replied u/Quantris.

u/Randomiser reminded everyone that "almost anagrams" were once a joke in the first run. The author had half forgotten. "One of my favorite moments of the whole experience."
