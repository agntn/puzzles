## Pizza day

May 22, 2019. Bitcoin Pizza Day, and the first block on the author's new subreddit, r/Grycoin. The plan was simple. Make a block easy enough for humans and bots alike, and see who's faster.

The question is one word: `Kraaz`. The format is `[Solution] TOMI [TOMI]`, and the TOMI field is "the relevant name for the solution". Digits `b5e`, `c` and `f`.

Atbash flips the alphabet. K becomes P, r becomes i, a becomes z. `Kraaz` is `Pizza`. And the relevant name? The man who paid 10,000 BTC for two pizzas, Laszlo Hanyecz.

## Nine minutes

The post went up at 12:57:25 UTC. The claim confirmed at **13:06:33**. "Even few minutes have not passed," one comment says.

u/mooncritic posted the string `Pizza TOMI Laszlo Hanyecz` and admitted the rush cost "a big fee". The chain backs that up. 7 mBTC went in, 6.36 went out, so almost 0.64 mBTC went to the miner. A fitting tip for pizza day.

The author took it as proof that humans beat scripts on easy blocks. Setting up a script takes time, hashing a pizza doesn't. The hardest part, by the author's own account, was spelling Hanyecz right.
