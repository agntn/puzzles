## No question

May 28, 2019. The question asks if you can solve a block without any question. Cute. The format wants a TOMI field, and the post gives the first digit `72e` and nothing else.

Players went straight for the paradox. `No Question TOMI No.` `without question`. `liar TOMI because it contains a question`. All of them fit the first digit, and none of them fit the hash.

u/silver_anth wasn't amused. A question should have one answer, they said, and a TOMI field just to make it harder is "wrecking this quizchain". The author disagreed and kept the field.

## The block number

A day later came the hint: "You know only the block number." Then a nudge. Might 21 mean something in the Bitcoin space?

Of course it does. `21 million`, the coin limit. The TOMI field is three lower case words, `fixed money supply`. After that hint it was just a race for the TOMI field.

The claim confirmed at **23:06:39 UTC** on May 29. u/silver_anth won and said sorry to everyone who spent hours on it. u/JDScreesh guessed `21 million` and got lost in the TOMI. English isn't their first language, and TOMI fields don't care.

u/puzzleponky had the best take afterwards. The block wasn't hard because of TOMI. Everyone just looked in the wrong direction, and a tiny nudge would have been enough.
