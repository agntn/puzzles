## The first Grycoin

June 11, 2019. The question: in what month will the first Grycoin be mined? The answer looks like `June 2019`. The TOMI field is a word and a number. Digits `56c`, `0` and `3c`.

Grycoin is the author's coin from the Wattpad story. The new chapter gave the year, 2020. But the month?

## Rapid fire

Hints every few minutes this time. The number has six digits and divides by 21. Then: mining will be outsourced to Bitcoin.

That's the halving. Block 630000, scheduled for May 2020. The answer is `May 2020`, the TOMI field `Halvening 630000`. Halvening with an n, like [block 18](/collections/quizchain2/18) of this run.

u/puzzleponky solved it five minutes after the second hint. The claim confirmed at **13:42:36 UTC**, 45 minutes after the post. And the halving? It came on May 11, 2020, at that very block.
