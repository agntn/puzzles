## A long time ago on a planet near to us

July 20, 2019, 11 mBTC. The hint came first, a day early, as a Wattpad chapter called "Waking Up". No question in it. The author funded the address half an hour before the post, just in case somebody solved it from the chapter alone. Nobody did.

The question sounds like Star Wars. `A long time ago on a planet near to us`. Digits `c65`, and later `04` for the solution alone.

## PETM

Guesses went everywhere. u/Quantris said the whole thing must be leading up to a reboot of _Dinosaurs_. Then they tried `first singularity TOMI wattpad chapter`. No dice.

The 24 hour hint never came. "Yes, forgot about that one", the author admitted. The next hint would come in the second stage of block 77. A day later, still no hint. "This time I did not forget about it."

Three days in, u/puzzleponky got it. `PETM TOMI second time now`. PETM is the Paleocene-Eocene Thermal Maximum, a warming spike some 55.5 million years ago. "Never heard of it until it was mentioned in the last chapter on Wattpad." A long time ago, on this very planet, it got hot. Second time now.

The claim confirmed at **05:30:04 UTC** on July 23. "I couldn't find the TOMI part", wrote u/JDScreesh. Honestly, who could?
