## Who this is

`@oritwoen` on X and GitHub. The same account that maintains this dataset, so yes, the record below was written by the side that won. Everything in it comes from the two public posts and the chain, not from memory.

The [Proof Of Writing essay](/collections/proof-of-writing) had been out since March 2025, with 5M XEC at first and 30M by August 2026. Two hints: `Revelations 22:13` and "It can be solved without ai or a computer". Alpha and Omega, the first and the last. So the answer starts at `Matter` and ends at the paragraph with "believe", and word n of paragraph n gives the twelve words.

## Why it held so long

The words were the easy half. The wallet is Cashtab, and Cashtab puts new wallets on `m/44'/1899'/0'/0/0`, not on eCash's registered coin type 899. The right phrase at the textbook path gives an empty address. A search that checks the textbook path first says no to the correct seed.

The claim went out on August 26, 2026 at 23:41 UTC. The announcement came the next day, quoting cain's relaunch post. It promised the solution in a few days, and it took a few hours: by then cain had released the seed, so the second post printed it with the method in three sentences.

## What the record says

Two facts, both from those posts: the announcement with the claim transaction, and the seed with the solution. The method itself sits on the puzzle, as the `answer` to the Revelations hint. No address: the record keeps the claim transaction, not where the coins went.
