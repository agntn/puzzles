## Who this is

A Reddit account that spent an evening on Kierkegaard_Soren's [trivia brainwallet](/collections/trivia-brainwallet) and won it. The claim landed at 02:05:31 UTC on November 5, 2017, and "Claimed it :)" went up in the thread eleven seconds after that timestamp. Half the prize went to charity, and the record keeps where: Quantris named `1HB5XMLmzFVj8ALj6mfBsbifRoD4miY36v`, WikiLeaks' donation address, and the spend pays exactly half the net amount there.

The best part is the honesty. Asked how they got number 12, Quantris said they "totally cheesed" it by trying even numbers on brainwallet.io until the address matched. Eleven right answers and a brute force salt. Then they posted all twelve answers anyway, steps included, and that walkthrough is where every `answer` on the record comes from.

## What the record says and what it doesn't

Three facts, one per comment: the claim, the guess on clue 12 with the WikiLeaks address, and the walkthrough. The record gives no addresses for Quantris. The other half of the spend went to `1Gfncuhg6hCQBxyrYsTDSwiCRzH7Bhjvru`, but nobody in the thread says whose it is, so it stays in the transaction and out of the solver.
