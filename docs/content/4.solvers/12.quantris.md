## Who this is

The Reddit account that ate Kierkegaard_Soren's [trivia brainwallet](/collections/trivia-brainwallet) in one evening. The claim landed at 02:05:31 UTC on November 5, 2017. Eleven seconds after that timestamp: "Claimed it :)". Short and sweet.

Half the prize went to charity. And not just any charity. Quantris named `1HB5XMLmzFVj8ALj6mfBsbifRoD4miY36v`, WikiLeaks' donation address, and the spend pays exactly half the net amount there. Clue 9 was a poem about WikiLeaks. Poetic justice, literally.

Now the best part. The author asked how they got number 12. Quantris just admitted it: they "totally cheesed" it, trying even numbers on brainwallet.io until the address matched. Eleven right answers and a brute force salt. Is that cheating? It's a brainwallet, the address is the referee. And then they posted all twelve answers anyway, with the steps. That walkthrough is where every `answer` on the record comes from. Legend.
