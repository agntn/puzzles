## Who this is

The account that started r/bitcoinpuzzles and posted its first puzzle. It's an [author](/authors/iamabananaamaa) first, and this page is the solver side of the same record.

The solve is [Wickex's puzzle](/collections/wickex/youtube), posted at 02:22 UTC on December 23, 2013, right after Wickex had taken IAMABananaAMAA's GIF. Hex that spells a YouTube link, Morse in the video, a short link in capitals, a second video with a string hidden in the sound, and that string as a brainwallet passphrase. It lasted about 45 minutes.

## The refund

Here is the bit that makes this page worth having. IAMABananaAMAA swept the prize and sent it straight back, in the same block, so somebody else could still play. Somebody did: the address was emptied again nineteen minutes later, and the thread never says who. Then the account posted the four steps under the puzzle for everyone who was still stuck. Solving a puzzle and handing the money back is not something you see often. Doing it to the person who solved yours an hour earlier is just good manners.
