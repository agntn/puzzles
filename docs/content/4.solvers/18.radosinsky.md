## Who this is

A handle at the end of the author's [solution post](https://x.com/VeteranHODL/status/2090400955707830480). "Congratulations to @\_radosinsky and thank you to everyone who took part". That's it. Who's behind it? The record can't say. When the Wayback Machine saved that post, X's own API couldn't even find the account behind the mention. So no profile link either.

## What they pulled off

Twelve photos, a paperback and a bit of arithmetic that wasn't arithmetic. [Hunting Time](/collections/hunting-time) hid most of its words as times in the plot, and the photos only told you which page to open. The final clue went up on August 13. The coins moved on August 18 at 02:07 UTC, in one transaction.

They missed the author's [before Eclipse bonus](https://x.com/VeteranHODL/status/2087583820836909270) by four days. He offered one anyway, that same day. Fair play.
