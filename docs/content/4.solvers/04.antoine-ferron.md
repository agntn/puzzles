## Who this is

A crypto developer living near the wall. CEO of BitLogiK at the time, says the bitcoin.fr intro. He solved [La Liberté guidant le peuple 2019](/collections/liberte-guidant-le-peuple) with Marina. On Twitter she was marabrito31. The author's solution names them both.

## How the week went

Monday evening, first visit. "Seed 12 words" in black on black. Then a street lamp's reflection showed writing next to Marianne's head. Phone torches brought out the rest. Base64 gave two words at once, and suddenly the list was French.

AES needed a key, so he emailed the artist. Caesar was annoying for a dumb reason: they kept reading `ATO` as `ATQ`. Wednesday evening, back with a blue LED torch for the colour strokes. Regex over the French list pinned four words. For the last two he wrote scanners. About 75 phrases a second per core, after some tuning. Then he cut the search to fifty words in ten orders.

Sunday, about 13:30 Paris time, a script called Seekplace found the phrase. He swept the coins with a fresh Electrum wallet. How do you wait an hour for a scan to finish? He doesn't say.

## The write-up

He told the whole thing to [bitcoin.fr](https://bitcoin.fr/recit-de-la-decouverte-des-bitcoins-dans-la-fresque-la-liberte-guidant-le-peuple-2019/) the next day. In French, wrong turns included. The scanners and the AES one-liner are public in [antonio-fr/pboywalltools](https://github.com/antonio-fr/pboywalltools). His closing line is the best part: Marina found a new passion, the command line.
