## Who this is

For years, just a handle in someone else's write-up. ZorenX wrote up the whole [Arweave series](/collections/weave) and names pogo as the solver of Puzzle 01 and Puzzle 02. That was the entire trail. Then the launch tweet of Puzzle #1 turned out to have a reply.

> Solved! The solution was "5 7 10:01 18 90 Dead Sea Bd6 Apple 039". Thank you very much for this puzzle, hoping more will come!

PogoB posted it on May 23, 2019 at 20:14 UTC. The prize wallet had moved at 19:53. Who else would have the full answer twenty minutes after the claim? ZorenX's write-up came a year later. So the record no longer guesses about a handle.

And the speed is the fun part. Puzzle 01 got its first AR on May 22. The page hit the chain the next afternoon, and Tiamat tweeted it at 18:33. Eighty minutes later the prize was gone. Seven riddles, logos included, typed in the exact format the page wanted. Puzzle 02 went up a day later and lasted under twelve hours. Two puzzles, 2250 AR. Good week.

## What the record says and what it doesn't

Three facts now: the reply, plus one per write-up. The Twitter account goes in as a profile and `PogoB` as an alias. The key stays `pogo`, the name ZorenX used. Is Pogo Bounce a real name? Nobody says, and the record doesn't either.

There's also a pogo writing puzzle write-ups on Medium around the same years. Tempting. But nothing on either account points at the other, so the record keeps them apart.
