## Who this is

A Reddit account with posts back to 2011. The record knows the handle and one night of it on r/bitcoinpuzzles, nothing more.

On December 23, 2013 at 01:01 UTC, Wickex posted the address of [IAMABananaAMAA's GIF puzzle](/collections/iamabananaamaa), two and a half hours after it went up. The prize moved three and a half minutes later. At 02:22 came [their own puzzle](/collections/wickex): hex, a YouTube video with Morse code, a second one with a string in the spectrogram, and a brainwallet. Then Wickex stayed in the comments and dropped hints until it was taken.

## What the record says

Two facts, both from Wickex's own posts that night. The solve comment on the GIF puzzle, and the post that says why the next one exists. Everything about the puzzle and who swept it lives on the collection page.
