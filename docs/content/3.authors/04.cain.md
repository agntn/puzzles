## Who this is

A writer on Proof Of Writing, the eCash site where a reader pays a writer in XEC to open the rest of a post. On X the account is Cain's Chronicles, `@caincurrency`. The byline on the essays is just cain.

The [puzzle](/collections/proof-of-writing) went up with an essay on March 1, 2025, on the first version of the site. Community, engagement, trust, a church on Sundays. The prize, 5M XEC, is only in the title. Nobody solved it, so on April 14, 2026 cain republished it on the new site with the address on top, 25M XEC promised and one more hint: it can be solved without AI or a computer. The X post that day said over 15M XEC was up for grabs. By the end of August the wallet held over 30M.

When it went, cain didn't hide it. The article opens with an update that the prize has been solved, a link to the solver's post and the solution behind the paywall, seed words bolded and capitalized. The cheapest way to check your own reading is 100 XEC.

## What the record says

Four facts. The first essay, cited to the Wayback capture from March 2025, because that capture is the essay as it first went out. The republication and its new hint, cited to the article on the new site. The relaunch post on X, archived with the other sources. And the solved update, from the same article. The deposits aren't facts here. They are on the puzzle record, with their transactions.
