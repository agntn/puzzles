## Who this is

A painter from Paris who signs as PBoy. In January 2019 he took Delacroix's Liberty Leading the People and moved it to the yellow vest winter. Same composition, same flag, new crowd. Then he hid a wallet in it.

[The puzzle](/collections/liberte-guidant-le-peuple) went up for the genesis block's tenth birthday, at 156 rue d'Aubervilliers. Coloured strokes, a black on black line, fluorescent ink you only see with a UV torch. Part of it worked from a photo. The rest wanted you on the pavement after dark. Street art that makes you go outside. Nice.

## Talking to the hunters

He answered a lot of comments under the r/Bitcoin post. Short replies, lots of emoji. Where is it? "Paris intra muros. And it's not very hard to find". Can I do it from home? "You can solve a part from your home". The day it was claimed he still didn't know who did it, and asked the winners to get in touch. That same evening the solution was up, in French and English.

## What happened to the wall

French authorities painted over it in February 2019. It stood for a few weeks. The hunt took six days of them. In September 2019 the mural came back as a set of 100 NFTs, according to his own page.
