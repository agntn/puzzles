## Who this is

Gem Finder by name, GemfinderOfX by handle. The [profile](https://x.com/GemfinderOfX) sums it up with an emoji: "I find Gems, if it's a gem I will find it and you will know 💎". That's all the record knows, and the handle stays a handle.

The puzzle post adds one line about the person: "I've been in Bitcoin for many years now. And I've loved it." The prize address backs that up a little. Its first coins arrived in January 2021, almost four years before the puzzle.

## One tweet, 0.02 BTC

[The 9vw04 puzzle](/collections/9vw04) is the one the record holds. One paragraph, one address, the token `9vw04` and a grid of coloured letters and digits. Everything is "within this message", and the author keeps saying so.

## How Gem Finder runs it

In the replies, one line at a time. Somebody guesses, the author answers. "WIF." "I don't really like rainbows." "Nope just your Brian and everything in the post." The brain one is spelled that way, and so is a lot of the rest. On purpose, the author says.

Then on January 13, 2025, a proper batch right under the puzzle. It's a WIF. Rainbows are a hint. Daniel Shanks, the creator of baby step giant step, is "very important here".

After that, fourteen months of quiet. In March 2026 one persistent player got a reply after all: every Nth character, maybe a skip cipher. The 0.02 BTC is still waiting. Who's counting?
