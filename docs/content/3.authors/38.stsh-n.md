## Who this is

A Reddit account. One post, zero comments, at least as far as the Arctic Shift archive of Reddit knows. That's the whole trail.

October 8, 2020, 09:25 UTC. [Brave New World](/collections/brave-new-world) goes up on the account's own profile, not in any subreddit. A collage of 2020, runes, "find the seed phrase", an address up the left edge. The title promises $2000. The address had held 0.2 BTC since May.

Then nothing. The thread kept growing for six years. The author never showed up in it.

## What the record says

Two facts, both straight from the post. It was a title and a picture with no text. The prize address is written into the picture. Everything about the puzzle itself lives on the collection page.
