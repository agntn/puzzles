## Who this is

A musician who makes downtempo electronica in a basement and hides wallets in it. The Farcaster bio says "Artist, Astrophysics BS, General Nerd, Builder." That's about the right order.

The albums are the puzzles. [Bifurcations](https://elronvhubbard.medium.com/logic-beach-bifurcations-album-puzzle-write-up-1e1094d41038) in 2020 had a Bitcoin prize and got solved. Two years later came the BITS EPther puzzle, which LogicBeach [describes](https://farcaster.xyz/logic-beach/0x05b52d78) as "2 songs, 2 minutes each, 2 music videos, in glorious 2-color", released on 2022/2/22 at 2:22:22. Commitment to the bit, literally.

Then [Powerful Moss](/collections/powerful-moss), the sixth album. The first plan, in April 2024, was a [wallet full of DEGEN and 33,333,333 MOSS](https://farcaster.xyz/logic-beach/0x778b3cc4), a meme token named after "my favorite bryophyte". By January 2025 it had turned into ETH on Base, held by a contract that also sells the album. Probably for the better.

## What the record says

Three facts, all from Farcaster. The March 2024 announcement with the Donkey Kong Country 2 soundfonts. The launch in January 2025. The prize a week later, "up to ~0.5 ETH and growing". What LogicBeach said about the puzzle itself, the baked in words and the promise of something "more creative", sits in the hints on the [puzzle record](/collections/powerful-moss), because it's about the puzzle, not about the author.
