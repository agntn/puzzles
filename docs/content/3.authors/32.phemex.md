## Who this is

Phemex is a crypto derivatives exchange, started in 2019. In January 2020 it ran the [Satoshi-Maze](/collections/satoshi-maze), a portrait with 2.1 BTC hidden in it. Big launch for a young exchange.

The pitch was key custody. The rules page talks up the exchange's HD cold wallet system and quotes the CEO, Jack Tao. A puzzle as an ad for cold storage. Odd, but it works.

## Max, the designer

The puzzle has a person behind it. [A Letter from Max](https://phemex.com/announcements/a-letter-from-max) opens with "I am Max, Phemex co-founder and the satoshi-maze puzzle designer". He signs the Ending too. Max who, though? The letters give a first name only, so the record keeps him as a fact on the exchange, not as a second author.

He also admits the puzzle "may have proven to be too tough to crack this time". Then he publishes the key himself. Respect for that.

## What the record says

Two facts. Max's letter, for who designed it. The rules page, for why an exchange hides keys in portraits. The dataset holds :dataset-count{author="phemex"} of theirs. The Ending promised "more challenges in the future". Will there be one?
