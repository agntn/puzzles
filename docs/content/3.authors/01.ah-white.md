## Who this is

A neurobiologist and bitcoiner, per the Nostr profile, and the author of _Walking Banks_. The novel is a murder mystery in Beijing with bitcoin as the motive. Agents chase a killer who harvests organs, because the victims carry a seed phrase in their DNA. Yes, really. A neurobiologist wrote it, so the genetics get a proper scene.

## Why give the book away?

The [free PDF post](https://njump.me/note1jvpc4m95fdr5hxqkhpyj5alcaeqj7hwtr6hdp4zk5n9uvewpzkyq2ldrlv) says it plainly. The book is meant to "orange pill" people. The ones who'd never pick up a bitcoin book but enjoy a good murder mystery. So the PDF went out free on Nostr in September 2024, and later the audiobook too.

And a real wallet went into the story. 800,000 sats, one day before the PDF. Then in May 2025 the author paid 28,000 sats in zaps to promote the book and the hunt. The [follow-up](https://njump.me/note16vcc0w99ul28qqru7wx8e9ajq2nx3tjujgfvv7rrysu34lm7knqqrlsmw3) counts two book sales from it. Honest numbers. How many authors post those?

## Does the author know the hunt is stuck?

Yes, and the hints say so. "It's 24 words" in May 2025, repetitions allowed in November, the words in the right order in March 2026. The last two open by admitting nobody has cracked it. The [collection page](/collections/walking-banks) walks through them, and through the xpub that made the wallet look empty.
