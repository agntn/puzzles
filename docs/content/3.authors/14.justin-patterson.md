## Who this is

A game developer who signs the hunt's page as jpatt94. Path to Greatness is his 3D platformer about parkour movement, and his own page says it's "currently being developed by just me, Justin Patterson". The full game was meant to be the demo plus themed levels, with a progression he compares to Super Mario 64.

He also wrote why the demo exists. The game was taking longer than planned, he didn't have the money to keep working on it, and he didn't want "another boring Kickstarter or Patreon page". So the demo got a [treasure hunt](/collections/path-to-greatness) inside, and the first site asked for donations to a second Litecoin address, half for the game and half for the prize.

## What the record says

Two facts, both from the first site, which still lives under `p2gtreasure.com/old/`. The game and the plan to hire a level designer with the donations. And the hunt itself, cited to the Wayback capture from February 2021, because that capture is the first site as it went out.
