## Who this is

Paul Jones is the name on the LuckyLurker author page and on the bitcointalk account that announced the [Vaults](/collections/luckylurker). The page says he started playing online slots in 2014, has tested hundreds of casinos, and has no formal credentials. LuckyLurker is a casino affiliate site. The Vaults are how it gets you to read the reviews: four of the first Vault's twelve hints send you to other articles on the same domain.

That's not a complaint. It's the context the puzzle makes most sense in, and it's why the second Vault has 1 BTC on it while the first had 0.8 mBTC.
