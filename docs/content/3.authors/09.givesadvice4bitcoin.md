## Who this is

A Reddit account. The record knows the handle and one morning on r/bitcoinpuzzles, nothing more.

On December 23, 2013 at 05:40 UTC it posted [the picture puzzle](/collections/picture-puzzle): a phone book, an eye, the letter O and a QR code, with 1 mBTC on the address. Then it stayed in the comments. "It's not a book though." "Try throwing the binary in your browser." When 6loss claimed the prize, the full solution went into the post.

## What the record says

Two facts, both from the author's own comments under the puzzle. A thank you to IAMABananaAMAA for starting the subreddit, and the congratulations to 6loss with the promise of the solution. The puzzle, the key and who took it live on the collection page.
