## Who this is

kTimesG is a BitcoinTalk handle and nothing more on the record. The account posts in the [Bitcoin puzzle transaction thread](https://bitcointalk.org/index.php?topic=1306983.0), and around this challenge its posts keep making one argument: "It's literally just one addition (sub) and one multiplication in the EC group to switch between any internal subinterval and a sequential interval." That was [the day after](https://bitcointalk.org/index.php?topic=1306983.msg64696210#msg64696210), to someone still asking how. The [80-bit challenge](/collections/ktimesg) was the argument made concrete: a key with 80 unknown contiguous bits hidden inside a 511-bit number, a public key that only existed in a pending transaction, and 0.005 BTC for whoever could do both parts fast enough.

It worked, sort of. The key fell, but slowly, and the author's post after the claim is more disappointed than proud: the spend waited 39 minutes before anyone replaced it.

## What the record says

The announcement, for the rules and the one request to the winner. The post after the claim, for the 39 minutes. Both are posts by kTimesG in the same thread, and each fact says in one sentence what its post says, with the post as the source. The winner's claim to the prize address is somebody else's post and sits on the collection page, not here.
