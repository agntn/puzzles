## Who this is

A BitcoinTalk account. Registered in December 2015, four posts in total, then nothing. The record knows the handle, the profile and one very short thread.

On January 12, 2019 at 10:33 UTC it posted [the Natasha Otomoski puzzle](/collections/natasha-otomoski). One question, two lines of rules, a signature and a txid. **1 BTC** was already on the address. Three minutes later the account was last seen. That's a mic drop.

## Who was blockladder, though?

Nobody knows. The thread had its suspects. morvillz7z was sure watashi-kokoto was blockladder all along. SirVladimir and GBT_User wondered whether the author took the prize back. None of that is on the record, because none of it has a source beyond a guess. The signature proves one thing only: whoever posted the question held the key.

## What the record says

Two facts. The thread opener, for the question and the signed prize. The forum profile, for the dates and the post count. The dataset holds :dataset-count{author="blockladder"} of theirs. Will there be a second one? akkort waited for it. Seven years on, still nothing.
