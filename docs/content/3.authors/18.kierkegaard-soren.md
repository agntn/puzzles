## Who this is

A Reddit account named after a Danish philosopher. That's the verified identity, and the record doesn't stretch it. In October 2017 r/bitcoinpuzzles was a ghost town, and the [announcement](https://www.reddit.com/r/bitcoinpuzzles/comments/77g5h7/meta_new_puzzle_in_development_005_btc_eta_is/) opens with "All (5 of you)". The pitch was a hunt of about 0.05 BTC, less technical than usual, so people from outside crypto could play too.

Two weeks later came the [trivia brainwallet](/collections/trivia-brainwallet). Twelve riddles, 0.04 BTC because the price had gone up in between, and a clue that asks for the year the author was born. The answer is 1813, which is Søren Kierkegaard's year, not the account holder's. Honestly, fair play.

It went fast. Quantris swept the wallet six hours after the post, and the author edited in a donation address for future puzzles and said "I want to make this a thing." Arctic Shift's copy of the account's posts has no second funded puzzle, so the dataset holds :dataset-count{author="kierkegaard-soren"} of theirs.

## What the record says and what it doesn't

Three facts, all from the account's own posts: the announcement, the launch at 0.04 BTC instead of 0.05, and the donation request in the edit after the claim. The donation address is on the record because the author names it. Nothing links it to the prize wallet, and the record doesn't pretend otherwise.

The name is a pen name, and the record treats it as one. No real name, no other platform.
