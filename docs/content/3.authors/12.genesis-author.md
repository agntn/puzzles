## Who this is

Nobody knows, and the record says so. The [Genesis puzzle](/collections/genesis) was announced in an OP_RETURN output in August 2026. Questions go in as transactions, answers come back as transactions, and better answers cost more sats. The author has a name on the collection, Anonymous, because the library wants a name and that's the honest one.

There's one thread worth writing down. An X account posted the announcement text, the target address and a hexdump of the Genesis block sixteen hours before the on-chain announcement, and went on answering questions under it. That's on the record as a fact about the account, because it's what the account did. It isn't a profile, because a profile is a claim that the author controls it, and prior knowledge of the announcement is evidence of a channel, not of a person.

The account kept posting. On September 5 it asked whether no one would be able to solve the puzzle. On September 18, a day after the author offered the public keys for 50k sats in `OP_RETURN`, it brought up the Coldcard incident and noted that this puzzle's pubkeys had not been exposed yet. On September 24 it posted a screenshot of 0xflorent's message to the puzzle address, ten minutes after the block that confirmed it. Close timing, again. Still a channel.

On October 6 it went full shop window. Who wants the two public keys? The encrypted ones are already on chain, the post says, next to the entropy clue. The image under it stacks the 50k offer, the terms, a paying player and the `BIE1` reply only that player can open. Good pitch. Same channel.

## What the record says

Six transactions and six posts. The announcement. The message offering paid hints. The September answer that named BIP39 with twelve words and a passphrase. The terms for the two public keys. The first message encrypted so that only one player can read it. And the X posts, including one about LLMs that find bugs and then fix them badly, which sits closer to the puzzle than it looks. One player says they brute force it with Claude. The last one asks who wants the keys. The address is the one the hint offer named for payments. Every fact here is a message the author wrote or a channel that carried it, nothing about who they might be.

If the wallet ever spends, the witness script becomes public and the collection page will change. This page won't, unless the author signs something.
