## Who this is

One letter. That's the whole handle. q grabbed it back when Stacker News was new and single letters were still up for grabs, and q was just the first random one nobody had taken. No hidden meaning, q says so in the thread. A bit disappointing for a puzzle author, but honest.

October 6, 2023. q posts [Brute force and the coins are yours](/collections/bitaddress). A bitaddress.org paper wallet from years back, BIP38 encrypted, passphrase gone. q already went through the old passwords and got nothing. So instead of letting the coins sit there forever, q hands them to anyone who can guess better.

And there's a message in it. The passphrase is "not an extra security layer", q says, just an extra step before the coins go to every bitcoiner. Pretty brave take on your own wallet. Also a great sales pitch for a puzzle, and the coins are still there, so the passphrase isn't exactly agreeing.

## What the record says

Three facts, from the post and one reply. The wallet with the forgotten passphrase. The point about passphrases. The story behind the name. What q remembers about the passphrase itself isn't here, that's hints on the [puzzle record](/collections/bitaddress), because it's about the puzzle, not about q.
