## Who this is

Gem Finder by name, GemfinderOfX by handle. In January 2025, the account went by IrrationalLibs. The [profile](https://x.com/GemfinderOfX) sums it up with an emoji: "I find Gems, if it's a gem I will find it and you will know 💎". The handle stays a handle.

The puzzle post adds one personal claim: "I've been in Bitcoin for many years now. And I've loved it." The prize address has history back to January 2021, but that doesn't establish who controlled it then.

## One tweet, 0.02 BTC

[The 9vw04 puzzle](/collections/9vw04) is the one the record holds. One paragraph, one address, the token `9vw04` and a grid of coloured letters and digits. Everything is "within this message", and the author keeps saying so.

## How Gem Finder runs it

In the replies, one line at a time. Somebody guesses, the author answers. "WIF." "Nope just your Brian and everything in the post." He uses pictures too: a Shanks meme, a rainbow GIF, a painted table.

On January 13, 2025, a batch appeared under the puzzle. It's a WIF. Rainbows are a hint. Daniel Shanks and baby step giant step are "very important here". Another note says "the spelling is incorrect on purpose". Which spelling does he mean?

Then a long quiet in the record. In February 2026 came "Grind", followed by the skip cipher clue in March. In November 2026 he was back in the replies, pictures and all. The prize was still unspent on November 27, 2026.
