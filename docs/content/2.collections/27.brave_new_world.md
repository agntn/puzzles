October 8, 2020, 09:25 UTC. The Reddit account stsh_n [posts to its own profile](https://www.reddit.com/user/stsh_n/comments/j79zvj/bitcoin_puzzle_2000/). The title is "Bitcoin puzzle (2000$)". The body is one picture and not a single word. The address holds **0.2 BTC**, and it had held it for five months already.

::

## What's in the picture

A collage, 1600 by 1200. "Welcome to the BRAVE NEW WORLD" in the middle, written with smaller handwriting around it that quotes the Bitcoin whitepaper. Behind that, faint and huge, "FIND THE SEED PHRASE". That's the only instruction the puzzle ever got.

Around it: George Floyd with "05.25.20" and "I can't BREATHE", Black Lives Matter slogans, surveillance cameras, a defaced statue, the pyramid from the Great Seal, a clock face. Trump and Biden face each other over "11.03.20" with a question mark between them. The Statue of Liberty holds a tablet marked BLM, with "1865-202…?" beside her and "ONLY real BITCOIN" on the pedestal. Lines of runes in four places: top left, down the right edge, along the bottom and next to the question mark. And up the left edge, rotated, `1KfZGvwZxsvSmemoCmEV75uqcNzYBHjkHZ`.

The BLM tablet and the slogans are why other lists call it the BLM puzzle, [floflo777's open-crypto-puzzles](https://github.com/floflo777/open-crypto-puzzles/tree/main/1-big-prizes/blm-brave-new-world-0-2btc) for one. The key here takes the title the picture gives itself.

## What the chain shows

The funding came first. 0.2 BTC landed on 2020-05-10 at 08:01 UTC, five months before the post. Then nothing until strangers started dropping small amounts: 1000 sat in October 2023, 100,000 in December 2024, 557 and 5727 in 2025. The address has received 20,107,284 sat and never spent any of it.

The thread has over two hundred comments by now. Rune translations, word lists, orderings, permutation counts. The [Arctic Shift](https://arctic-shift.photon-reddit.com/) archive of Reddit has no comment from stsh_n under any of them, and the coins haven't moved.

### No hints

The author published a title and a picture, so the record has no hints. The rune readings in the comments are guesses nobody confirmed, and a hint records who gave it, not who guessed.

### Started at the post, funded before it

`startedAt` is the post, because that's when anyone could start. The funding transaction stays on the record with its own date, five months earlier. The four top-ups are `increase` entries. `prize` stays 0.2, the amount the author put there.

### The picture

`assets/brave_new_world/puzzle.png` is the file Reddit serves for the post, 1600 by 1200. `assetUrl()` points at it on GitHub.

### A singleton id, no name segment

`brave_new_world` is the whole identifier, like [`picture_puzzle`](/collections/picture_puzzle), [`gsmg`](/collections/gsmg) and the other singletons. One puzzle, one key, no slash.
