## The puzzle

Zden publishes images with a key hidden in them, on [crypto.haluska.sk](https://crypto.haluska.sk/). A level is an image. The prize sits on the address. The solver posts the key. The series moved across four chains, so this is the one collection that uses every chain factory except Arweave's and Monero's. `zden/decred_janus` paid 460 DCR, `zden/litecoin_segwit` 230 LTC, `zden/xixoio` 6 ETH.

## What the records carry

Every Zden record has `assets({ puzzle: "…png" })` pointing at the image under `assets/zden/` in the repository, so `assetPath()` and `assetUrl()` answer for all fifteen. Thirteen carry the public key, three carry the private key. The rest were solved with the key never posted. They're `solved` on the author's word, with the claim transaction on the record and `verifyPuzzle` answering `unavailable`.

The hints Zden published are on the records too, in the shape they came in. Codex Protocol got two more renderings, Litecoin SegWit three SVG drawings and Decred Janus a stripped down tree, so those three list the files under `assets({ hints })` and `puzzles_show` prints them as `hint assets:`. Demobit and XIXOIO were hinted on Twitter, so those two carry `official(…)` entries with the tweet as the source and a Wayback capture behind it. `puzzles_hints` answers the text ones. The image ones are files, and `assetLinks()` is where a script picks them up.

```ts [src/collections/zden/decred_janus.ts]
export const zdenPuzzleDecredJanus = decredPuzzle({
  id: "zden/decred_janus",
  address: p2pkh("DsRaAja82UvgnqYaBHYFuyCKURFX2rCyEJ8"),
  sourceUrl: "https://crypto.haluska.sk/decred_tree.svg",
  startedAt: "2017-03-07 04:20:56",
  status: Status.Solved,
  pubkey: compressed("02278753666ec31c29b755a39421017bd80744d728204da0f7ff452846112b618b"),
  prize: 460,
  solvedAt: "2017-03-18 13:51:29",
  transactions: [
    funding("900e…05f2", "2017-03-07 04:20:56", 100),
    increase("47c3…60c2", "2017-03-07 04:31:20", 100),
    increase("f968…6581", "2017-03-07 04:48:32", 100),
    increase("2a06…e90e", "2017-03-07 20:38:42", 150),
    increase("327a…4b13", "2017-03-07 20:55:54", 5),
    increase("77f5…4719", "2017-03-07 21:00:08", 5),
    claim("2af4…5467", "2017-03-18 13:51:29", 460),
  ],
  assets: assets({ puzzle: "decred_janus/puzzle.svg" }),
});
```

`prize: 460` is in DCR because the factory is `decredPuzzle` and `prizeCurrency()` reads the chain's symbol from `@agntn/chains`. A `currency` field is only written when the prize isn't the native token.

## Status

Two puzzles are still open, both on Bitcoin and both small: `zden/level_5` and `zden/level_halv`. `zden/level_5` is the one with a story. The 2018 image was incomplete and the author relaunched it on 12 December 2021 with a corrected one and new hints. The record quotes that statement and the 2018 hint tweet, so `puzzles_hints` on `zden/level_5` says which image to work from. `zden/level_halv` is the halving puzzle from April 2024, and the only thing the author said about it, that it is way easier than level 5, is on the record as well. The rest is solved. Want to see what a solved multi chain record looks like? This is the collection.
