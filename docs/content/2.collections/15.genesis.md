The [announcement](https://mempool.space/tx/b691de3657880d9a1eabd2783b1a9fa8c5313ced338495bf10e85727012d7a77) was confirmed on August 22, 2026, in block 963629. Its author says the wallet uses only information from Bitcoin's Genesis block and has extremely low entropy.

::

The record is `genesis/block`. Its address is `bc1qfkhx02v89u2qyyyljeczw6hu9sr437y44t7ae5yf09thrdukfqesnjg2wj`, a P2WSH address. The library stores it as `standard`, not `p2wpkh`: the witness script and public keys are not recorded.

## What the sources establish

The record preserves nineteen messages through September 19. Earlier replies spend change outputs linked back to the announcement. The [September 19 follow-up](https://mempool.space/tx/d78593ab6288e2c9792f8c5ad8d020893d1b1dd9631eb5b1ffa5765f13029cd7) uses the announcement's input address again. Each hint links to its transaction and records the attribution evidence. Relayed player questions are not author hints.

The replies describe a 2-of-2 multisig, BIP39 with twelve words and a passphrase, and BIP48. Later messages call the passphrase a name and the entropy a 128-bit digest. These are the announcer's claims, not a verified derivation method. The earlier "no hash" answer remains as published rather than being silently rewritten to fit the later digest clue. No public identity is established for the author.

An [August 23 message](https://mempool.space/tx/248f690de194372564baa14e1bebf08154e2f2042ed205baa6157fdc0e3f22ea) offers hints in exchange for payments to this address. Receiving a message does not prove who wrote it. The record therefore does not attribute every incoming OP_RETURN to the author. Hint payments accumulate at the target, but the record leaves the changing prize amount unset. Use the balance command for the current amount.

No key, solution, claim transaction or fixed prize is recorded. The start date is the announcement's block timestamp, not the earlier first deposit.
