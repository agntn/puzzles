March 1, 2025. Proof Of Writing, the eCash site where readers pay writers in XEC, publishes [Building an awesome eCash community and a chance to win 5M XEC](https://web.archive.org/web/20250327234214/https://proofofwriting.com/building-an-awesome-ecash-community-and-a-chance-to-win-5m-xec/) by cain. Three minutes of reading about size, engagement, trust and a church on Sundays. No riddle anywhere. The chance to win is in the title and that's it.

The wallet behind it is a Cashtab wallet, and the 5,000,000 XEC landed the same morning. Nobody found it. By August 2026 the prize was **30,068,000.94 XEC**, and on August 26 it left in one transaction.

## Two hints

The first one was a Bible verse, `Revelations 22:13`. "I am Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the end, the first and the last." Looks like decoration on an essay about community. It's the whole trick.

The second came with the relaunch. On April 14, 2026 cain moved the article to the new Proof Of Writing, put the address on top, raised the prize and wrote under the old hint: "It can be solved without ai or a computer". 10M more went in that morning, another 10M in June and 5M on August 20. That last one lasted six days.

## The diagonal

Alpha and Omega is the first and the last. The first word of the puzzle is `Matter`, the opening of the second paragraph, and the last paragraph that counts is the one that says "people who show up because they believe". Twelve paragraphs from matter to believe. Then walk down the diagonal: word 1 of the first, word 2 of the second, word 3 of the third, all the way to word 12 of the twelfth.

| Paragraph opens with                                             | Word       | BIP39     |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | --------- |
| Matter of fact                                                   | Matter     | `matter`  |
| Another key to                                                   | key        | `key`     |
| Engagement is easily                                             | easily     | `easily`  |
| This isn’t a slot machine                                        | slot       | `slot`    |
| Like timber from a maple tree                                    | maple      | `maple`   |
| Purpose and belief may be two                                    | two        | `two`     |
| If eCash wants to take on Visa                                   | Visa       | `visa`    |
| Trust is crucial. Lately, crypto has been swamped                | swamped    | `swamp`   |
| On a personal note, my wife has been subjecting                  | subjecting | `subject` |
| We mostly keep to ourselves, and have made no friends            | friends    | `friend`  |
| I appreciate how everything runs so smoothly. They have a robust | robust     | `robust`  |
| It’s what the eCash community could be, I thought. What a trip   | trip       | `trip`    |

Nine words are already on the BIP39 list. Three are not, `swamped`, `subjecting` and `friends`, and each one drops its ending to get there. The checksum passes too, and a random twelve gets that one time in sixteen. Finding the words takes no computer at all, so cain wasn't bluffing.

## Path 1899, not 899

The phrase alone is not the wallet. eCash's registered coin type is 899, and `m/44'/899'/0'/0/0` gives an address nobody ever funded. Cashtab has made every new wallet on `m/44'/1899'/0'/0/0` since [March 2024](https://reviews.bitcoinabc.org/D15679), and that's the one that holds the prize. A search that trusts the coin type throws the right answer away, the same trap as the [Movie Enigma](/collections/movie-enigma) checksum, just one level down.

[Ori](/solvers/oritwoen) got it, swept 30,067,980.76 XEC on August 26, 2026 at 23:41 UTC and posted the next day. cain's update on the article says the prize has been solved and links that post. The seed and the method went public the same afternoon.

### The first eCash record

`ecashPuzzle` puts it on the `ecash` chain. The address is CashAddr under the `ecash:` prefix, amounts are XEC with two decimals, and `verify()` derives the seed at the recorded path, 1899 included. Run `puzzles verify proof-of-writing` and it answers `OK`.

### A round trip in September

On September 29, 2025 the wallet sent 5,059,096.41 XEC out and got 5,058,997.76 XEC back thirteen hours later, both sides carrying the XECX token. Only the key holder could sign the first one, so it's a `decrease` and not a claim. The coins came home and the puzzle stayed open.

### What the transactions leave out

Sixty-eight deposits of 1,000 XEC reached the address too, most in March and July 2025, all but one with a `PAY` marker in the OP_RETURN. They're small and nobody announced them as prize money, so the record lists the four prize deposits and the round trip, not every payment. `prize` is what the claim transaction spent, which includes all of them.

### The text

`assets/proof-of-writing/puzzle.txt` is the essay as the [Wayback Machine saw it on March 27, 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20250327234214/https://proofofwriting.com/building-an-awesome-ecash-community-and-a-chance-to-win-5m-xec/): the title and the fourteen paragraphs, nothing else. The version on the new site sits behind a 100 XEC paywall, with the seed words bolded and capitalized.
