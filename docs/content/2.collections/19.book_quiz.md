One day after the [birthday quiz](/collections/satoshi_birthday_quiz) fell in five hours, AoiNakamoto ran the format again, this time in [r/YangForPresidentHQ](https://www.reddit.com/r/YangForPresidentHQ/comments/b9zg9p/7_million_book_quiz_challenge_to_this_subreddit/), with the same **7,000,000 satoshis** and a stopwatch. Seven multiple-choice questions about a campaign book, sixty minutes from the moment the questions went up, and if nobody swept the address in time the money went home. Nobody did.

## How the book quiz works

### Seven questions, four of them opinions

The recipe is identical to the birthday quiz: paste the correct answer sentences together with one space between them, SHA-256 the result, feed the hex to a brain wallet tool as entropy, and the first address of that wallet holds the prize.

What changed is the questions. Three of them are facts you know if you read the book: who killed the 1971 basic income bill, how the book pays for the income, what it says about the Green New Deal. The other four are the author's taste. "The correct answer will just be whatever I personally feel is the best" is in the post, before the questions, so nobody can say they were not warned. Question 7 asks for a better title for the book, and the winning option is "Challenge Fucking Accepted.", which lands only if you notice the word challenge in the post's own title.

The book is never named. "Which book? You know already, if you are reading this." The questions are about universal basic income, a value added tax, Humanity First and robots taking jobs, and one of the wrong titles is "Normal Strikes Back", so it is Andrew Yang's _The War on Normal People_. The record keeps the thread and leaves the identification here, where it can be argued with.

### An hour is not a lot

The birthday quiz ran until someone won. This one had a deadline: sweep it within sixty minutes or the author takes the funds back. With four subjective questions in the set, the honest strategy was brute force, and the author later admitted as much, counting two pure luck questions and "still leaves many possible combinations".

The chain says how that went. The address was funded at **02:58:10 UTC** on April 6, 2019 and emptied at **04:53:28**, one hour and fifty-five minutes later, by the author. A commenter did find the key afterwards and posted the seven sentences in full; AoiNakamoto confirmed them, explained which questions had no objective answer, and said sorry for the format. Second experiment, second lesson.

## What the book quiz record carries

### Expired, with the answer key on the hint

```ts [src/collections/book_quiz.ts]
export const bookQuizPuzzle = bitcoinPuzzle({
  id: "book_quiz",
  address: p2pkh("1PLa3c2xjtoP6YE1FvaLhsf4akSxvdv3Ta", "f5064148351fc664b483e20a58b0742312c0e03d"),
  sourceUrl: THREAD,
  startedAt: "2019-04-06 02:58:10",
  status: Status.Expired,
  pubkey: compressed("02c879abfb2a6b24940ded09bbf421ace2f328a012244960a9d8ef407d73f3d7b4"),
  key: seed("have piano caution aim ... brother job", "m/44'/0'/0'/0/0").entropy(
    "69b4809282a49a05138839a04aa87c7f20545ff060fc0c2a11e8b0e4cfda873b",
    source(THREAD, "SHA-256 of the seven answer sentences, joined by single spaces"),
  ),
  prize: 0.07,
  transactions: [funding(/* … */), decrease(/* … */)],
});
```

`expired` is the status for a prize the author took back, the same one the two unsolved [WarpWallet challenges](/collections/warp) carry. The spend is a `decrease`, not a `claim`, because nobody collected anything: 0.06978813 BTC went to the author's own P2SH address and 21,187 satoshis went to the miner.

The six hints are all AoiNakamoto talking, in the order the post reads: the rules, the warning about opinion questions, the deadline, the refusal to explain any further, the update that the money was gone, and the post-mortem on which questions were unfair. The answer key hangs off the first hint as its `answer`, because that is where it was published. A hint never says whether it was right.

## Where the address comes from

### The thread never printed it

Same as the birthday quiz: no address, no txid, anywhere in the post or the comments. What the author did publish is the recipe and, afterwards, the answers. Hash the seven sentences, paste the hex into a BIP39 tool, read the first address at `m/44'/0'/0'/0/0`, and you get `1PLa3c2xjtoP6YE1FvaLhsf4akSxvdv3Ta`.

Two things confirm it. The chain: 7,000,000 satoshis in and out again inside two hours, on the right day, one funding and one spend, and the spend exposes the public key that hashes to the address on the record. And the money's provenance: the funding came straight out of `1B3X6xQxSGQYkE4JMR1BwbZ4S5J99SWd2V`, which is the change address of the transaction that funded the birthday quiz the day before. Same wallet, same author, two quizzes.

`verify()` reruns the second half on every check, so the record proves its address instead of asserting it.

### A singleton id, no name segment

`book_quiz` is the whole identifier, like [`gsmg`](/collections/gsmg), [`bitaps`](/collections/bitaps), [`mineshop`](/collections/mineshop), [`movie_enigma`](/collections/movie_enigma) and [`satoshi_birthday_quiz`](/collections/satoshi_birthday_quiz). One puzzle, one key, no slash.
