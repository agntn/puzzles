December 21, 2024, 04:05 UTC. Gem Finder, GemfinderOfX on X, [posts a puzzle](https://x.com/GemfinderOfX/status/1870319691295764655). "Today I wanted to do something special." Then 0.02 BTC, about $2,000 at the time, and one promise: "Everything for the private key can be found within this message."

The message isn't long. A paragraph, an address, five odd characters and a picture. Almost two years later the coins haven't moved.

## What's in the 9vw04 post

### A grid that is very much a rainbow

The picture shows 21 rows of 50 characters on black. Letter, digit, letter, digit. The letters run through all 26 in one fixed order, starting with T. Colour goes by pair, not by character. Most pairs are a white letter and a pink digit. M and G pairs are blue, N pairs lavender, O pairs grey. Five colours, then. The last row sits on a thin line at the bottom edge, with no margin under it. The first row has one above it. So the grid may go on past the picture.

The author's [December 22 reply](https://x.com/GemfinderOfX/status/1870866790752141328): "I don't really like rainbows." A colourful way to say that.

### 9vw04, the typo that isn't

`9vw04` sits right after "showing proof of funds", like a slip of the thumb. On January 2 the author [showed a private key example](https://x.com/GemfinderOfX/status/1874632897459871930) with it twice:

```text
******9vw04********************9vw04
```

"All 5 of these numbers and letters appear 2 times in the private key example." The mask is 36 characters, though. A WIF has 51 or 52. Base58 has no `0`, so `9vw04` can't appear in a WIF as written. The author also said "the spelling is incorrect on purpose".

In November 2026 he confirmed it's a zero, not an `o`. He also said "yes it appears as is in the WIF". A player pointed out the Base58 problem. The author agreed, then said: "The main idea of this is not taking things at face value." What do you make of that?

### An address with a past

`bc1qr5ms…` isn't fresh. It got its first coins in January 2021. The same key spent from it in October 2021 and again on November 28, 2024, 23 days before the post. What was left, 2,020,760 sats, became the prize. "Proof of funds", as the post says.

Those spends exposed the public key, so the record carries it. Useful, but not a solution. The author has published no reduced key range in the posts cited here.

### Baby step giant step, with just your brain?

On January 6, the author posted a Daniel Shanks meme. Its caption: "THE FACE YOU MAKE WHEN PPL OVERLOOK THE OBVIOUS". A week later, he called Shanks and baby step giant step "very important here".

Four days before that answer, someone asked whether software or a brute force script was needed. "Nope just your Brian and everything in the post". The posts don't explain how the clues fit together. Treat Shanks as a clue, not an instruction to start a key search.

### Fourteen months later, a skip cipher

After the January 2025 batch, the first sign of life in the record came on February 13, 2026. "The Bitcoin is still there. All the clues are too. Grind"

On March 1, [every Nth character had something to say](https://x.com/GemfinderOfX/status/2027938957112279215). There "could be" a fixed interval skip cipher. Which N, and which sequence? He doesn't say. The grid offers 1,050 characters, but the post doesn't say it's the sequence.

### November 2026: the rainbows come back

On November 16, someone asked for a small spark. The author replied with a rainbow GIF over clouds. Was it a new hint? "Yes".

On November 24, he wished players luck and said "I’ve seen a handful of you guys making some leeway." The picture showed a rainbow gradient table and a white plate marked `#`. Does a rainbow table with a hash on it ring a bell?

Later that day, he said "the main thing is utility of the repeated hints “🌈”". The trail goes back to December 2024, through January's rainbow remarks and November's pictures. For someone who doesn't like rainbows, he keeps sending them.

## Is the 9vw04 puzzle solved?

No. On November 27, 2026, the address still held 2,020,760 sats in two unspent outputs. Nothing has left it since November 2024. The prize is still there and the record remains `unsolved`.

The record keeps all four transactions of the address, from the 2021 funding on. The coins are almost four years older than the puzzle, so `preGenesis` is set and the start is the post itself.
